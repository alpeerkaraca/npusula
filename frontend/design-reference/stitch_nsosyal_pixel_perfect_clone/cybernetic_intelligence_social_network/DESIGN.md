## Brand & Style
This design system pairs the rapid density of a next-generation professional social network with the sharp analytical clarity of an AI decision engine. Built for forward-thinking operators, data analysts, and executive teams, the interface projects analytical precision, fluid connectivity, and cognitive speed.

The aesthetic fuses **Dark Glassmorphism** with **High-Contrast Data Modernism**. Deep abyssal slate backdrops eliminate visual fatigue during sustained operational use, while luminescent electric blue accents and spectral gradient glows draw instant focus to high-value signals, decision trees, and emergent network trends. Elements do not compete for attention; rather, structural information retreats into quiet tonal depths, allowing real-time AI recommendations and social feeds to stand out with distinct luminance and deliberate authority.

## Colors
The color architecture enforces a high-contrast dark environment crafted to maintain readability across continuous feeds and dense data matrices.

### Foundations & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0b1118`)**: Deep obsidian base for the overall window and viewport canvas.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#131c27`)**: Structural modules, left navigation bar, persistent cards, and input backgrounds.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1a2533`)**: Interactive hover states, active feed layers, and dropdown containers.
- **Subtle Surface (`#0f1721`)**: Recessed areas, secondary wells, and inactive tab bars.
- **Border / Structural Lines (`#1d2b3a`)**: Subdued border delineation maintaining shape clarity without eye friction.
- **Border Glow / Active (`#00a3ff40`)**: Translucent electric blue highlight applied to active cards and focused interactive components.

### Typography & Iconography Tokens
- **Text High-Contrast / Primary (`#f1f5f9`)**: Primary headlines, metric readouts, and main user content.
- **Text Secondary (`#94a3b8`)**: Explanatory body text, metadata, timestamps, and secondary labels.
- **Text Muted (`#64748b`)**: System icons, subtle counters, placeholder text, and structural dividers.

### Strategic Accents & AI Intelligence
- **Electric Blue (`#00a3ff`)**: Primary interaction point, active indicator pills, and focused icons.
- **Cyan Surge (`#00d2ff`)**: High-light terminal points, gradient stops, and real-time AI pulse states.
- **AI Core Gradient**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #0091ff 0%, #00d2ff 100%)` reserved for decisive actions, intelligence generation triggers, and active module highlights.

### Signal Badges & Decision Health
- **High Potential / Success (`#10b981`)**: Emerald green signaling verified positive sentiment, strong model confidence, and target acquisitions.
- **Medium Potential / Caution (`#f59e0b`)**: Amber warning for borderline metric triggers, watchlists, and pending decisions.
- **Low Potential / Alert (`#ef4444`)**: Coral red denoting critical social anomalies, algorithmic divergence, or system errors.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies exclusively on **Inter** to ensure uniform structural integrity, fast scanning, and consistent rendering across complex analytical data streams and mobile screens.

- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`)**: Mandatory for all numerical indicators, decision scores, percentages, and financial or interaction counters to prevent layout shift during real-time streaming.
- **Headings & Hierarchy**: Large headlines employ tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em`) to build an authoritative, editorial anchor.
- **Label Tokens (`label-sm`)**: Used for system status badges, recommendation tags, and AI confidence parameters; rendered in semi-bold uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.04em`) for legibility against dark surfaces.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements a structural 3-column asymmetric canvas on desktop environments:

1. **Left Primary Navigation (Fixed, 260px - 280px)**: Hosts the persistent brand header (N Beta), main network pathways, direct messaging shortcuts, notification triggers, and the dedicated decision support beacon.
2. **Center Dynamic Stream (Fluid, max-width 720px)**: Feeds, interactive decision cards, generative synthesis views, and discussion threads.
3. **Right Contextual Deck (Fixed, 320px - 360px)**: Real-time decision metrics, algorithmic watchlists, trending insights, and query consoles.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Flow
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: Complete three-column experience. Margins set to `space-xl` (`2rem`) with structural gutters of `1.25rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: Contextual right deck condenses into drawer sheets; left rail collapses to a 72px icon-only vertical shelf with expanded tooltip popovers. Center stream claims fluid breadth.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single-column full-width feed. Fixed left navigation switches to a streamlined bottom utility rail with elevated quick-action triggers. Outer canvas margin snaps to `margin-mobile` (`1rem`).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy opaque shadows, using dark optical layering, translucent glassmorphism, and neon radiance.

- **Level 0 (Baseplate Canvas)**: Flat `#0b1118`, establishing absolute floor depth.
- **Level 1 (Structural Rail & Persistent Shell)**: `#131c27` combined with a `1px` structural outline in `#1d2b3a`. Used for persistent sidebars, top headers, and static panels.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Overlay Glass)**: Semi-transparent `#131c27cc` with a `12px` to `16px` backdrop-blur filter and subtle rim lighting (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Level 3 (Floating AI Popovers & Context Menus)**: `#1a2533` with elevated dark perimeter drop (`0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`) and a `1px` border of `#2a3b4e`.
- **AI Accent Glow (Active State)**: When the decision engine highlights a card, an ambient radial blur is injected beneath the element (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 163, 255, 0.25)`), instantly isolating key recommendations from passive items.

## Shapes
A dual-radius paradigm bridges structured social feeds with human-centric AI interactions:

- **Structural Cards & Panels**: Default to `rounded-lg` (`12px` / `0.75rem`) to create clean, architectural divisions for data streams, comment sections, and metric graphs.
- **Action Elements & Buttons**: Default to full capsule pill rounding (`border-radius: 9999px`) or `12px` (`0.75rem`) for standard inputs, maintaining a tactile, inviting touch.
- **Interactive Badges & Meta Chips**: Scaled to fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) profiles to distinguish metadata from content structures.
- **Media & Avatar Shapes**: Profile avatars use circular rings (`9999px`) with an outer offset ring of `2px` colored `#0b1118` to cleanly separate images from neighboring backgrounds.

## Components

### 1. Left Navigation Bar & Brand Pillar
- **Brand Identifier**: Features the high-contrast `N Beta` badge. The letter "N" uses a bold gradient fill (`#0091ff` to `#00d2ff`) against a `#131c27` squircle base, paired with a small `#00d2ff` status dot.
- **Navigation Items**: Composed of `#94a3b8` text, an icon with standard `20px` stroke, and dynamic background fill. On hover: text transitions to `#f1f5f9` on a background of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **AI Module Selector (Active State)**: The module button features an electric blue compass icon enclosed in an active container. It is styled with an inset border highlight, a soft neon background tint (`rgba(0, 163, 255, 0.1)`), an electric blue active indicator bar along the left edge, and full-contrast text `#f1f5f9`.
- **Counter Bubbles (Notifications / Messages)**: Minimalist pill badges filled with `#00a3ff` or `#ef4444` for critical alerts, using white text at `label-sm` with `tnum` alignment.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary / Decision Action**: Rounded pill (`9999px`) or refined `12px`, featuring the AI core gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0091ff 0%, #00d2ff 100%)`). Text is crisp bold `#0b1118` or pristine white `#ffffff` depending on contrast mode. Subtle ambient drop glow on hover: `0 4px 14px rgba(0, 163, 255, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary / Neutral Action**: Dark container `#131c27`, border of `1px solid #1d2b3a`, text `#f1f5f9`. On hover, border shifts to `#00a3ff` and background brightens to `#1a2533`.
- **Ghost / Utility**: No background, borderless, text `#94a3b8`. Transitions smoothly to `#f1f5f9` with a subtle wash on hover.

### 3. Indicator Chips & Decision Badges
- **General Badge Structure**: Height `24px` to `28px`, pill radius (`9999px`), horizontal padding of `space-sm` (`0.5rem`).
- **High Potential / Success**: Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, text `#10b981`. Includes a leading `6px` solid emerald indicator dot.
- **Medium Potential / Caution**: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`, text `#f59e0b`.
- **Low Potential / Warning**: Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`, text `#ef4444`.

### 4. Feed & Decision Cards
- **Card Chassis**: Surface `#131c27`, border `1px solid #1d2b3a`, border-radius `12px`, padding `space-lg`.
- **AI Recommendation Injection**: When an insight card appears within the social feed, it is accented with an ultra-fine gradient top line (`linear-gradient(90deg, #0091ff, #00d2ff)`), paired with a translucent compass watermark placed in the top-right corner.
- **Card Footer Metrics**: Engagement counters, comment counts, and algorithmic share triggers use `#64748b` typography, turning to `#00a3ff` on hover.

### 5. Input Fields & Decision Prompt Terminals
- **Input Field**: Height `44px`, corner radius `12px`, background `#0b1118`, border `1px solid #1d2b3a`, text `#f1f5f9`, placeholder `#64748b`.
- **Interactive Focus State**: Transition to border color `#00a3ff` with an outer focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 163, 255, 0.15)`.
- **AI Command Prompt Box**: Multi-line contextual input equipped with a gradient-tinted submit beacon, automated prompt suggestions styled as rounded pill chips, and clear model status indicators.

### 6. Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radio Buttons)
- **Checkbox**: `18px × 18px`, `4px` corner radius, border `1px solid #1d2b3a`, background `#0b1118`. On check: background switches to the AI gradient with a white checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons**: `18px × 18px`, circular (`9999px`), styled with an inner active dot glowing in `#00a3ff`.
